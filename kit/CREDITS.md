# Hyrule kit — credits and licenses

Fan art inspired by The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo.

- **Art**: every image in `img/` was newly generated for these family learning apps (AI-assisted painting, then cut out, cleaned and
  resized by hand-written scripts). No sprites, screenshots, logos or official art were used as inputs.
- **Music and sound** (`music.js`): original compositions, synthesized live with WebAudio. They share only instruments and mood with the
  game; no Nintendo melody is quoted or adapted. `ocarina.js` is a free-play instrument with no built-in songs.
- **Fallback skin** (`fallback/`): original names and simple SVG drawings, for use if the fan-art theme ever has to be switched off.
